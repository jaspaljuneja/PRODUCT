## 🔍 The Problem

> **76%** of Americans live paycheck to paycheck.
> **Only 46%** have 3 months of emergency savings.
> **Nearly 40%** have less than $500 in cash savings.

Millions of everyday people struggle financially — not because they don't earn enough, but because they lack a simple, unified tool to understand their money, control their spending, and start building savings.

Existing financial apps fail this audience in one of three ways:
- Too complex for non-experts
- Too narrow in scope (budgeting *or* investing, never both)
- Show data but don't recommend the next step of action

The result: people feel anxious about money, avoid thinking about it, and never build savings or the right mindset around them.

---

## 👥 Who It's For

**Primary users:** Everyday earners across all age groups — anyone who receives a regular paycheck and struggles to make it last or grow.

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Household Income | $30,000 – $90,000/year |
| Financial Literacy | Low to moderate |
| Behavior | Reactive — spend first, save what's left (often nothing) |
| Emotional State | Stressed or avoidant about finances |
| Tech Comfort | Moderate — comfortable with apps, not financial platforms |

**Not building for:** High net-worth individuals, finance professionals, or businesses.

### Pain Points

**Emotional**
- Guilt around spending
- Anxiety about having no emergency fund
- Feeling "behind" financially

**Practical**
- No clear picture of where money goes each month
- Savings feel impossible when bills consume the whole paycheck
- Investing feels too intimidating
- Nothing connects the full financial picture in one place

**Behavioral**
- Budgets get abandoned within weeks
- Apps show data but don't guide action
- No small wins to build momentum

---

## 📣 Why Now

Mint — one of the most popular personal finance apps — **shut down in 2023**, leaving approximately **3.6 million users** without a replacement. This audience is still actively searching for a simple, trustworthy alternative.

No current app combines simple budgeting, savings goals, and beginner investment guidance in one jargon-free experience with actionable recommendations. That's our opening.

## ✨ Features

Features are prioritized using MoSCoW across v1, v2, and beyond:

### ✅ Must Have (v1 Launch)
| User Story | Description |
|---|---|
| **US-01** — Bank account connection | Core value prop. Without it the app has no data and can't function. |
| **US-06** — Safe to spend number | Single most differentiating feature. High impact, low build effort. |
| **US-05** — Overspend alert | Drives daily engagement via simple push notifications. |
| **US-04** — Spending breakdown | Core budgeting view. Users expect this — no differentiation without it. |
| **US-07** — Savings goals | Primary retention driver. Users who set a goal are 3× more likely to return. |

### 🔵 Should Have (v1)
| User Story | Description |
|---|---|
| **US-03** — Jargon-free onboarding | Critical for new users. High effort but protects day-1 retention. |
| **US-02** — Variable income setup | Necessary for users with irregular income. Without it, they churn immediately. |
| **US-09** — Investing 101 content | Low effort to produce. Builds trust before asking users to invest. |

### 🟡 Could Have (Post-launch)
| User Story | Description |
|---|---|
| **US-08** — Micro savings suggestion | Adds delight but not a blocker for v1 launch. |
| **US-10** — Retirement indicator | High value but complex to build accurately. Better in v2. |

### 🔴 Won't Have (v1)
| User Story | Reason |
|---|---|
| **US-11** — Investment recommendations | Requires regulatory compliance (financial advice laws). Too risky for v1. |

## 🏆 Competitive Landscape

|---|---|---|

| Copilot | Beautiful UI, smart tracking | No investing, premium price |

| Huntington | Visual spending breakdowns | Doesn't recommend or prioritize next actions |

**Our edge:** The only app that combines jargon-free budgeting, savings goals, and beginner investment guidance — with clear, personalized recommendations on what to do next.

## 💡 Product Hypothesis

> *"We believe that everyday earners who live paycheck to paycheck need a single, jargon-free app that connects their spending, saving, and investing — so they can take small, confident steps toward financial stability without feeling overwhelmed."*

**We'll know it's working when:**
- Users open the app **3× per week** on average
- **40%+** of users set up a savings goal within the first week
- Users report feeling **"more in control"** after 30 days (in-app survey)
